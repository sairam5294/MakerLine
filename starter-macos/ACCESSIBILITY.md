**Our goal:** everyone can use this app, including people who use VoiceOver, a keyboard or
Switch Control, need larger text or higher contrast, or are sensitive to motion. We aim to
meet **WCAG 2.2 level AA** (applied to a native app) and Apple's accessibility guidelines.

## What we do
- Every button, checkbox and pop-up menu has a spoken name and role (tested on every change).
- Everything works from the keyboard: Return = Done, Esc = Not now / close, Tab moves between
  controls, Cmd+, opens Preferences, Cmd+Q quits.
- Clickable targets are at least 44 × 44 points.
- Text and controls meet at least 4.5:1 contrast (3:1 for borders and focus rings) in light
  and dark appearance; Increase Contrast makes secondary text and borders stronger still.
- State is shown in words (e.g. "Reminders paused"), never by colour or icon alone.
- Nothing flashes. With Reduce Motion on, windows appear without animation.
- Text uses Apple's text styles and wraps instead of being cut off.
- No account, no network, no analytics. Settings stay on your Mac.

## Known limitations
- macOS has no system-wide text-size setting for third-party AppKit apps; use Zoom
  (System Settings → Accessibility → Zoom) to enlarge everything. _Update this list after
  each manual VoiceOver test (see README → Accessibility checklist)._
