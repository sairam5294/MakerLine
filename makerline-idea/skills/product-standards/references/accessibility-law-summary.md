# Accessibility Law — summary for the idea stage

_Condensed from `accessibility-law.md` (last verified 2026-10-19, Ontario 2026-10-20; not legal advice). Cite this file in EVALUATION §11. Uncertain + high stakes (public-sector buyers, EU consumer services, US public accommodations, health or finance) → "confirm with a lawyer" + a CLARITY question. Deep runs and `ship-checklist` read the full file._

**Baseline everywhere:** WCAG 2.2 AA (= ISO/IEC 40500:2025), accessible worldwide, no discrimination (`inclusion.md`). It meets the WCAG level every law below cites.

| Market | Law | Who it binds | What a small product must do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ontario | AODA (IASR) | Every org with ≥ 1 employee in Ontario (customer service); 50+ staff (web) | Policies, publicised feedback route, accessible formats on request (timely, no extra cost), notice of service disruptions, training on IASR + Human Rights Code for staff and contractors; 20+: compliance report (next due 31 Dec 2026); 50+: written policies, multi-year plan, WCAG 2.0 AA |
| Canada federal | Accessible Canada Act | Federal and federally regulated buyers | EN 301 549 conformance + ACR when selling to them |
| USA private | ADA Title III | Public accommodations (websites and apps in practice) | WCAG 2.1/2.2 AA, accessibility statement, real screen-reader testing, no overlays; lawsuits rising (3,117 in 2025) |
| USA public | ADA Title II | State/local government, schools and their vendors | WCAG 2.1 AA + ACR; deadlines 26 Apr 2027 (≥ 50k pop.) / 26 Apr 2028 |
| USA federal | Section 508 | Federal agencies' purchases | ACR on the ITI VPAT template |
| EU | European Accessibility Act | Consumer e-commerce, banking, e-books, telecoms, transport ticketing — any seller into the EU | Since 28 Jun 2025: EN 301 549, accessibility info (Annex V), complaints route. Service microenterprises (< 10 staff, ≤ €2M) exempt |
| EU / UK public | WAD · PSBAR 2018 | Public bodies and their suppliers | WCAG 2.1 AA (EU) / 2.2 AA (UK), statement, feedback route |
| UK private | Equality Act 2010 | All service providers | Accessible service and alternatives on request (WCAG 2.2 AA de facto) |
| India | RPwD Act 2016 | Government and private service providers | GIGW 3.0 / IS 17802 (≈ WCAG 2.1 AA); accessible KYC alternatives; expect ACR requests; CCPD fines rising |
| Australia | DDA 1992 | Anyone providing goods or services | WCAG 2.x AA, alternatives, complaint route |
| Japan | Disability Discrimination Act (amended 2024) | All businesses | Respond to accommodation requests; core flows work with assistive tech |

**Privacy travels with it:** PIPEDA + provincial health laws (Ontario PHIPA) · HIPAA + CCPA · GDPR / UK GDPR · India DPDP 2023 · Australia Privacy Act · Japan APPI. Health, children's and financial data raise the bar everywhere.

**Solo-founder checklist:** WCAG 2.2 AA · accessibility statement · feedback route with a non-email option · alternate formats on request · ACR when selling to organisations · accessible identity/KYC · honest Apple accessibility labels · test records · a customer's contract clause applies even when your size exempts you.
