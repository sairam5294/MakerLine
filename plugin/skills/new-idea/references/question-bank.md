# Discovery Question Bank

Pick adaptively; don't ask everything. **C** = Core, **O** = Optional (ask it, or record a labelled default). **D** = decision (offer a recommended option first + one-line trade-off) · **F** = fact (never recommend an answer; add "I don't know yet") · **open** = free text with an example, not multiple choice.

## A. Problem & goal
- (C·F·open) What problem are you solving, and for whom? Describe a real moment when someone feels this problem.
- (C·F) How do people solve it today? (other apps, WhatsApp groups, YouTube, paper, asking family, nothing)
- (C·F) Why is now the right time / why you? (skills, network, personal experience, content access)
- (C·D) What is the goal of the project? Options: learning/portfolio · side income · full business/startup · community/social impact · internal tool
- (C·D) What does success look like in 6 months? Pick one primary metric: active users · paying customers · revenue · engagement (e.g. daily minutes) · a specific outcome
- (O·D) What is explicitly NOT a goal?

## B. Target audience
- (C·F) Who is the primary user? Age range, role, location/language, tech comfort, device.
- (C·D) Who pays, if different from the user? (e.g. parents pay, kids use; company pays, employees use)
- (C·F) Where do these people already spend time (communities, apps, events, influencers)? — needed for distribution.
- (O·F) Secondary users (admins, content creators, teachers)?
- (O·F) Accessibility needs likely in this audience (older adults, low literacy, low vision, children)?
- (O·D) Languages needed at launch vs later?

## B2. Alternatives & differentiator (hypotheses only — evaluate-idea researches the facts)
- (C·F·open) Who or what are you up against? Name apps, sites, communities, people doing it by hand — and "doing nothing".
- (C·F·open) What do people dislike about those today? (a real complaint you've heard or seen beats a guess)
- (C·F·open) Why would someone switch to yours? Finish the sentence: "Unlike ___, we ___." — the differentiator hypothesis.
- (C·F) What would it cost a user to switch (data to move, habits, people to bring along, money already paid)?
- (O·F) Why hasn't a bigger player done this already? (too small for them, needs local knowledge, regulation, they tried and stopped)
- (O·F) Is there an unfair advantage only you have here? (access to users, content, a community, a skill, lived experience)

## C. Scope & platforms
- (C·D) Platforms: web app · iOS · Android · all three · web first then mobile. Recommend based on audience (e.g. Indian mass market → Android first).
- (C·D) The 3–5 things a user must be able to do in version 1 (MVP). What's the ONE core action?
- (C·D) Nice-to-haves for later?
- (O·D) Offline use needed? Real-time features (chat, live)? Media (audio/video)? Notifications? Location? Camera?
- (O·D) Admin panel / content management needed?
- (O·D) Integrations (payments, calendars, WhatsApp, maps, AI)?

## D. Experience & design
- (C·D) What should the product feel like? Pick 3 adjectives (e.g. calm, playful, premium, trustworthy, fast).
- (C·D) Design framework preference: platform-native (Apple HIG + Material 3) · custom brand design system on a component library (shadcn/ui, Tamagui, NativeWind) · existing brand/design system · no preference (recommend).
- (O·F·open) Reference products you love (and what specifically)?
- (O·F) Existing brand assets (logo, colours, fonts)? Figma files?
- (O·D) Light/dark mode, themes for kids, cultural motifs?

## E. Technology
- (C·D) Any technology preferences or constraints? Options: no preference — recommend · must use X · avoid X.
- (C·D) Who will build it? Only AI tools + me · me + a freelance developer · a dev team later.
- (O·F) Existing accounts/tools: GitHub, Vercel, Supabase, Firebase, Apple/Google developer accounts, Figma?
- (O·D) AI features planned? What would AI do for the user?
- (O·F) Expected scale in year one (hundreds, thousands, lakhs of users)?

## F. Business & constraints
- (C·D) Monetization: free · freemium · subscription · one-time purchase · ads · donations/sponsorship · B2B licensing · not decided.
- (C·F) Budget for tools/hosting/stores per month, and one-off budget (e.g. ₹0, ₹2k, ₹10k+). Note: Apple developer account ≈ US$99/yr; Google Play one-time ≈ US$25.
- (C·D) Timeline: target date for a first testable version; hours per week you can spend.
- (O·F) Personal data sensitivity: children, health, finance, location, photos?
- (O·F) Legal/regulatory concerns (content rights, religious/cultural sensitivity, copyrighted music)?
- (O·D) Launch market(s): India only · specific cities · global diaspora · worldwide.

## G. Naming
- (O·D) Do you have a name? Should names be English, Sanskrit/Telugu/Hindi-inspired, invented, descriptive?
- (O·D) Must the .com / .in domain or matching app-store name be available?
- (O·D·open) Words or tones to avoid?

## Follow-up patterns
- Vague audience ("everyone") → "Who are the first 100 people you'd hand this to? Where would you find them?"
- Big scope → "If you could only ship one feature in 4 weeks, which one?"
- Unclear value → "What would make someone stop using their current workaround?"
- Unclear success → "What number, in 6 months, would make you happy to continue?"
- "No competitors" → "What do people do today instead, even if it's clumsy? That's the competitor."
- Feature-list differentiator ("more features", "AI-powered") → "Which one thing would a user notice in the first minute, and why can't the leader copy it in a month?"
- Pays question → also ask what they pay for today (tools, commissions, ads) — the best willingness-to-pay signal before any test.
- End users vs customers → if the user serves their own customers (bakers → buyers), ask whether those customers touch the product (link, web page, nothing).
- Owner unsure → log it in CLARITY.md as an open question with how to find out; don't push for an answer they don't have.
