# New Idea — Discovery

1. Get the pitch (2–5 sentences); restate in one line; confirm. Keep the first reply short (≤ 150 words + round-1 questions).
2. Read `docs/product/OWNER.md` (skills, hours, budget, accounts, preferences) and pre-fill from it and the pitch — never re-ask, only confirm. Ask for missing OWNER.md fields once and offer to save them to the kit's `owner/OWNER.md`.
3. Interview in 4–6 rounds of 3–5 questions from `references/question-bank.md`, order: problem/goal → users → scope/platforms → design → technology → business → naming. Multiple choice (AskUserQuestion if available). **Decisions** (goal, platform, scope, stack) get a recommended first option; **facts** about users/market (what they use today, what happened) never do — a "recommended" fact is a leading question. Core questions required; optional ones get a labelled default.
4. Offer technology suggestions, not just questions: give a provisional recommendation based on answers so far (final choice happens in tech-stack).
5. Make vague answers testable ("everyone" → "who are your first 100 users and where are they?").
6. Capture the owner's unfair advantages.
7. Write `docs/product/PROJECT_BRIEF.md` from `references/brief-template.md`; label assumptions; list open questions.
8. Show a 6–8 line summary, get confirmation, set STATUS Discovery: Done, offer evaluate-idea.

No critique in this phase — that's evaluate-idea.
