# Accessibility Law by Market

_Last verified 2026-10-19. Not legal advice: it tells you which law to look at and what it usually asks for. Items marked **unverified** were not confirmed against an official source in the last check — confirm before relying on them. `standards-refresh` re-checks this file; the kit's own baseline (`inclusion.md`: WCAG 2.2 AA, accessible worldwide, no discrimination) applies everywhere regardless._

## How to use
1. List the launch markets **and** where paying customers are (a buyer's law flows down through contracts even when you are exempt).
2. Find each row below; copy "What a small product must do" into EVALUATION §11 and PRD §9.
3. Uncertain + high stakes (public-sector buyers, EU consumer services, US public accommodations, regulated finance) → "confirm with a lawyer" + a CLARITY question.

## Data-protection law in the same markets (the privacy row of EVALUATION §11)
Accessibility and privacy obligations arrive together. Check `privacy.md` §2 and, by market: Canada **PIPEDA** (federal private sector) and provincial health-information laws — Ontario **PHIPA** for anything touching patient data (clinics are "health information custodians"; vendors sign agent agreements) · USA **HIPAA** for covered entities and their vendors (business associate agreements), plus state laws (CCPA/CPRA) · EU/UK **GDPR / UK GDPR** · India **DPDP Act 2023** · Australia **Privacy Act 1988** (APPs) · Japan **APPI**. Health, children's and financial data raise the bar everywhere; uncertain + high stakes → confirm with a lawyer.

## Standards and app stores
- **WCAG 2.2** is the W3C Recommendation (Oct 2023). **WCAG 3.0** is a Working Draft with no completion date — never plan compliance against it. ISO/IEC 40500 = WCAG 2.0, not updated (**unverified**).
- **Apple Accessibility Nutrition Labels** (App Store pages, OS 26+): nine declared features (VoiceOver, Voice Control, Larger Text, Dark Interface, Differentiate Without Color Alone, Sufficient Contrast, Reduced Motion, Captions, Audio Descriptions). Voluntary now; Apple says they will become required, date unannounced. Misleading labels are actionable under App Review Guideline 2.3 — claim only what you tested.
- **Google Play:** no equivalent label programme found (**unverified**).

## What a solo founder does, and which law asks for it
1. **WCAG 2.2 AA** — satisfies the 2.0 / 2.1 AA levels every law above cites. (AODA 50+, ADA II/III practice, 508, EAA / EN 301 549, UK, India, Australia)
2. **Accessibility statement** — standard met, known gaps, contact, date. (EAA Annex V, UK public sector, EU WAD; expected in any ADA defence and under AODA)
3. **Feedback / complaint route** in accessible formats: email plus a non-email alternative. (AODA customer service from 1 employee, EAA, WAD, UK, ACA)
4. **Alternate formats and communication supports on request.** (AODA, UK Equality Act, Australia DDA, Japan)
5. **ACR on the VPAT template** when selling to organisations. (US federal and state/local, EU and UK public sector, Canadian federal buyers; emerging in India)
6. **Accessible identity / KYC flows** — alternatives to liveness or blink checks. (India: Pragya Prasun, SEBI)
7. **Apple labels filled honestly**, with test evidence per claimed feature.
8. **Keep test records and technical documentation.** (EAA Annexes IV–V; any ADA defence)
9. **Contract flow-down:** a customer's WCAG / EN 301 549 clause applies even when your own size exempts you (AODA < 20 / < 50 staff, EAA microenterprise).

## Changed recently — watch
- US Title II deadlines moved to 26 Apr 2027 / 26 Apr 2028; more DOJ rulemaking expected.
- US Title III lawsuits at their highest since 2022.
- EAA: first court order (June 2026); no fines yet; Dutch enforcement expected late 2026; EN 301 549 V4.1.1 (WCAG 2.2) awaiting Official Journal citation — then WCAG 2.2 becomes the EU baseline.
- AODA compliance report due 31 Dec 2026.
- India: mandatory ICT standards and ACR portal still draft at last check; CCPD fines escalating; check for a DEPwD/MeitY notification and any RBI circular.
- Apple labels may become mandatory.
- Australia's 2025 AHRC guidelines make WCAG the reasonable baseline.
