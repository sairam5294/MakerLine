# Discovery Question Bank

Pick adaptively; don't ask everything. **C** = Core, **O** = Optional.

## A. Problem & goal
- (C) What problem are you solving, and for whom? Describe a real moment when someone feels this problem.
- (C) How do people solve it today? (other apps, WhatsApp groups, YouTube, paper, asking family, nothing)
- (C) Why is now the right time / why you? (skills, network, personal experience, content access)
- (C) What is the goal of the project? Options: learning/portfolio · side income · full business/startup · community/social impact · internal tool
- (C) What does success look like in 6 months? Pick one primary metric: active users · paying customers · revenue · engagement (e.g. daily minutes) · a specific outcome
- (O) What is explicitly NOT a goal?

## B. Target audience
- (C) Who is the primary user? Age range, role, location/language, tech comfort, device.
- (C) Who pays, if different from the user? (e.g. parents pay, kids use; company pays, employees use)
- (C) Where do these people already spend time (communities, apps, events, influencers)? — needed for distribution.
- (O) Secondary users (admins, content creators, teachers)?
- (O) Accessibility needs likely in this audience (older adults, low literacy, low vision, children)?
- (O) Languages needed at launch vs later?

## C. Scope & platforms
- (C) Platforms: web app · iOS · Android · all three · web first then mobile. Recommend based on audience (e.g. Indian mass market → Android first).
- (C) The 3–5 things a user must be able to do in version 1 (MVP). What's the ONE core action?
- (C) Nice-to-haves for later?
- (O) Offline use needed? Real-time features (chat, live)? Media (audio/video)? Notifications? Location? Camera?
- (O) Admin panel / content management needed?
- (O) Integrations (payments, calendars, WhatsApp, maps, AI)?

## D. Experience & design
- (C) What should the product feel like? Pick 3 adjectives (e.g. calm, playful, premium, trustworthy, fast).
- (C) Design framework preference: platform-native (Apple HIG + Material 3) · custom brand design system on a component library (shadcn/ui, Tamagui, NativeWind) · existing brand/design system · no preference (recommend).
- (O) Reference products you love (and what specifically)?
- (O) Existing brand assets (logo, colours, fonts)? Figma files?
- (O) Light/dark mode, themes for kids, cultural motifs?

## E. Technology
- (C) Any technology preferences or constraints? Options: no preference — recommend · must use X · avoid X.
- (C) Who will build it? Only AI tools + me · me + a freelance developer · a dev team later.
- (O) Existing accounts/tools: GitHub, Vercel, Supabase, Firebase, Apple/Google developer accounts, Figma?
- (O) AI features planned? What would AI do for the user?
- (O) Expected scale in year one (hundreds, thousands, lakhs of users)?

## F. Business & constraints
- (C) Monetization: free · freemium · subscription · one-time purchase · ads · donations/sponsorship · B2B licensing · not decided.
- (C) Budget for tools/hosting/stores per month, and one-off budget (e.g. ₹0, ₹2k, ₹10k+). Note: Apple developer account ≈ US$99/yr; Google Play one-time ≈ US$25.
- (C) Timeline: target date for a first testable version; hours per week you can spend.
- (O) Personal data sensitivity: children, health, finance, location, photos?
- (O) Legal/regulatory concerns (content rights, religious/cultural sensitivity, copyrighted music)?
- (O) Launch market(s): India only · specific cities · global diaspora · worldwide.

## G. Naming
- (O) Do you have a name? Should names be English, Sanskrit/Telugu/Hindi-inspired, invented, descriptive?
- (O) Must the .com / .in domain or matching app-store name be available?
- (O) Words or tones to avoid?

## Follow-up patterns
- Vague audience ("everyone") → "Who are the first 100 people you'd hand this to? Where would you find them?"
- Big scope → "If you could only ship one feature in 4 weeks, which one?"
- Unclear value → "What would make someone stop using their current workaround?"
- Unclear success → "What number, in 6 months, would make you happy to continue?"
