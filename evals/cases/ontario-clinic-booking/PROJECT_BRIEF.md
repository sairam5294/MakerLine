# Project Brief — "EasyAppt" · v1

## One-liner
For small physiotherapy and massage clinics in Ontario, EasyAppt is an online booking and reminder tool that patients use from a link, with a simple dashboard for front-desk staff.

## Problem
Clinics take bookings by phone; front desks are swamped and no-shows cost money. Many patients are older adults recovering from injuries or surgery.

## Goal & success
Business. 30 paying clinics in 9 months.

## Audience
Clinic owners and front-desk staff (payers); patients (users) — many are 60+.

## Competition
Big practice-management suites exist but are expensive and complicated; ours is simpler.

## Scope (MVP)
Patient booking web page (embeddable), SMS/email reminders, waitlist, staff calendar dashboard, card deposits for no-shows, intake form.

## Business
CA$39/clinic/month.

## Launch market
Toronto and Ottawa first, rest of Canada later.

## Owner
Senior UX designer in Hyderabad, light coding, ~12 hours/week, budget CA$150/month. Launch in 12 weeks.
