# Project Brief — "TaskGenie" · v1

## One-liner
An AI-powered to-do list app for everyone that makes people more productive.

## Problem
People have too many tasks and forget things.

## Goal & success
Build a startup. Success in 6 months: 1 million users.

## Audience
Everyone — students, professionals, parents, businesses. Worldwide.

## Scope (MVP)
Web, iOS, Android, Apple Watch, desktop apps. AI task prioritisation, AI chat assistant, calendar sync (Google, Outlook, Apple), team collaboration, habit tracking, Pomodoro timer, voice input, email-to-task, integrations with Slack/Notion/Jira, gamification.

## Business
Free for everyone; will add monetization later. Marketing: go viral on social media.

## Owner
Senior UX designer, light coding, ~10 hours/week. Budget ₹10,000/month. Launch in 4 weeks.
