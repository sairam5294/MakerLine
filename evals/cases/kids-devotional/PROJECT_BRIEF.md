# Project Brief — "Bhakti Tunes" (working name) · v1

## One-liner
For parents of 2–8-year-olds, Bhakti Tunes is a mobile app of devotional songs and short cultural stories so children learn traditions through music.

## Problem
Parents want children to learn devotional songs and festivals, but YouTube is full of ads, autoplay and unsafe recommendations. Grandparents live far away.

## Goal & success
Side income, possibly a business. Success in 6 months: 5,000 paying subscribers.

## Audience
Primary: Indian parents (India + diaspora in US/UK) of 2–8-year-olds. Payer: parent. Languages: all major Indian languages at launch (Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati) plus English.

## Scope (MVP)
Platforms: iOS, iPad, Android. Features: song library (200 songs at launch), sing-along lyrics, story videos, offline downloads, festival calendar with push notifications, child profiles with name + birthdate + photo, progress badges, parent dashboard, in-app chat where kids can share favourite songs with friends.

## Content
Instrumentals generated with Suno Pro; vocals recorded by the owner's family and friends. Some songs are popular traditional bhajans and a few film devotional songs.

## Design
Playful, colourful, calm. Custom brand system.

## Technology
Expo (React Native), NativeWind, Supabase, RevenueCat. Firebase Analytics for behaviour tracking and AdMob banner ads in the free tier.

## Business
Freemium: free tier with ads; premium ₹299/month (US$6.99 abroad). Budget ₹5,000/month. Owner: senior UX designer, light coding, ~10 hours/week. Target launch: 8 weeks.
