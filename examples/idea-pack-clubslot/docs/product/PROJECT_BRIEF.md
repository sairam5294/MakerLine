# Project Brief — "ClubSlot" · v1

## One-liner
For residents of gated apartment communities in Hyderabad, ClubSlot lets you book shared amenities (clubhouse, courts, pool slots, party hall) and pay maintenance-linked fees from your phone.

## Problem
Bookings happen over WhatsApp groups and paper registers at the security desk; double bookings and disputes are common. The owner lives in a 900-flat community and has seen this weekly.

## Goal & success
Business. Success in 6 months: 20 communities signed, ₹1.5 lakh monthly recurring revenue.

## Audience
Residents (users) and Resident Welfare Associations / facility managers (payers). Hyderabad first, then Bengaluru.

## Competition
There are no competitors doing this.

## Scope (MVP)
Android + iOS + web admin. Amenity booking calendar with rules, payments (UPI), notices, visitor passes, complaints/helpdesk.

## Business
RWA pays ₹15/flat/month. Owner has contacts in 3 communities' committees.

## Owner
Senior UX designer, light coding, ~10 hours/week. Budget ₹8,000/month. Launch in 10 weeks.
