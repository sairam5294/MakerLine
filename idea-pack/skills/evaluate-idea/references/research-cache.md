# Research cache format — docs/product/research/<topic>.md

Save research once, reuse it across refinement rounds, tools and phases (tech-stack, PRD, post-launch).

```
# <topic>  (e.g. competitors, market-size, pricing, regulations)
| Date checked | Fact | Source URL | Type (evidence/estimate) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-09-28 | Kutuki charges ₹399/month | https://… | evidence |
```
Rules: one fact per row · always a URL · re-check facts older than 30 days before relying on them · never store personal data.
